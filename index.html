Hypoallergenic & Non-Comedogenic Products

+

We select foundations, primers, and powders that do not clog your pores (non-comedogenic) and are safe for extremely sensitive or acne-prone skin, preventing breakout flare-ups after your events.

Ultra Long-lasting & Sweat-proof Lock

-

With Nepal's warm seasons, makeup longevity is vital. We utilize high-performance primers, baking techniques, and setting sprays that resist sweat, humidity, and tears for up to 18+ hours without creasing.

Skincare-Infused Foundations

+

Our luxury bases contain nourishing active ingredients like Hyaluronic Acid, Vitamin E, and Glycerin. They actively lock in moisture, giving you a plump, dewy look rather than a dry, cakey texture.

HD Camera & 4K Flash Proof

+

Cheaper cosmetics create a white flashback reflection in photos. We use fine silica-free translucent powders and micro-pigment foundations that absorb light properly, making you look stunning in high-definition photographs.
